# Triage — Senior Digital Companion

A calm, senior-friendly mobile web app designed to help older adults review messages, spot scams and AI-edited images, organize schedules, and enhance photos with large buttons and plain language.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Home Hub
Shows one important decision at a time with sender name and email; taking any action (**Confirm**, **Save to**, **Dismiss**, or **Ask Family**) automatically brings up the next message.

### 2. Unified Inbox
Connects read-only Gmail and Outlook accounts with automatic background email checks, plain-language AI summaries, priority sorting, 1-tap **Save to** filing, and customizable categories (**Personal** and **All** always kept).

### 3. Scam Shield
Checks pasted messages or screenshots with AI and safety rules to give a clear **Safe**, **Caution**, or **Danger** verdict, paired with 4 Golden Safety Habits and a Family Code Word.

### 4. AI Media Check
Scans uploaded photos and email image attachments for AI-generation signs with honest results (*AI signal found*, *Camera signal found*, or *Cannot tell*), plus a 4-Spot Eye Check Guide and Voice Clone Safety tips.

### 5. Photo Helper
Enhances photos in one tap (**Make Clearer**, **Brighten Dark Photo**, **Fix Old / Scratched Photo**, **Tidy Background**) with a Before/After preview while always saving edits as a new copy.

### 6. Smart Calendar & Paper Calendar Snap
Automatically suggests plans from messages with accurate categories (**Medical**, **Family & Social**, **Bills & Errands**, **Daily Routine**) and turns photographed paper calendars into reviewable reminders.

### 7. My Data & Privacy Controls
Keeps undismissed data stored locally on-device with clear ON/OFF toggles for every source, AI Reading, and Family Sharing, a transparent **"What AI Read"** log, backup download, and two-step erase.
