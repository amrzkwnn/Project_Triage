# Senior Digital Companion (`Build_Bersama`)

A calm, easy-to-use mobile web app designed to help senior citizens stay safe, organized, and confident on their phones—without confusing menus, tiny text, or technical jargon.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Home Hub
- One calm starting screen showing **"Today's Important Decisions"** (such as a clinic appointment waiting to be saved, or a suspicious message needing a family check).
- Five large, clearly labeled buttons to open each tool, plus a visible **My Data & Privacy** status pill and a persistent **"← Back to Home"** button on every page.

### 2. Unified Inbox (Email + WhatsApp + Prescriptions)
- **All Important Messages in One Place:** Emails arrive automatically; WhatsApp, SMS, social messages, and prescription photos can be forwarded or pasted in a couple of taps (including via iPhone **Share > Actions**).
- **Clear Source Labels:** Every message clearly shows where it came from (*From Email*, *From WhatsApp*, *From Photo*, etc.) with an icon and text label.
- **Simple Sorting:** Organizes messages into **Personal**, **Family**, and **Others**, while putting **Important** items ahead of **Social** chats and **Promotions**.
- **Plain-Language Summary:** Turns long or messy messages into 1–2 clear sentences showing who sent it and what action (if any) is needed.
- **1-Tap Next Steps:** Forward appointments straight to your **Calendar**, send suspicious texts to **Scam Check**, inspect group-chat photos in **AI Media Check**, or **Ask Family** to review.

### 3. Scam Shield ("Is This Safe?")
- **Instant Safety Check:** Paste a suspicious message or upload a screenshot to get a clear **Safe (Green)**, **Caution (Yellow)**, or **Danger (Red)** answer with simple reasons—without ever opening risky links.
- **4 Golden Safety Rules:** Always visible on one screen (*Urgency*, *Secrecy*, *Unusual Payment*, and *Hang Up & Call Back on Saved Number*).
- **Family Secret Code Word & Safe Phone Checklist:** Set a private family code word to verify emergencies and check off basic privacy steps like silencing unknown callers.

### 4. AI Media Check (For Group Chats)
- **Spot Fake Photos:** Checks uploaded group-chat images for AI-generation signals and gives an honest result (*AI signal found*, *Camera signal found*, or *Cannot tell* when chat apps strip photo details).
- **4-Spot Eye Check Guide:** Teaches four quick things to inspect yourself (*Hands & Fingers*, *Words & Signs*, *Eyes, Teeth & Glasses*, and *Smooth Plastic Faces*).
- **Voice Clone Safety:** Reminds seniors that short video clips can mimic a loved one's voice and encourages verifying by calling their saved phone number or asking for the Family Code Word.

### 5. Photo Helper (One-Tap Fixes)
- **Four Simple Buttons (No Sliders):** **Make Clearer**, **Brighten Dark Photo**, **Fix Old / Scratched Photo**, and **Tidy Background**.
- **Original Photos Stay Safe:** Shows a simple **Before / After** comparison and always saves edits as a **new copy**, never overwriting the original memory.
- **Old Print Scan Tip:** A 3-step guide for photographing printed album photos without glare.

### 6. Systematic Phone Calendar & Paper Calendar Snap
- **Snap Your Paper Calendar:** Take a photo of a paper wall calendar and review detected dates **one by one** with **Keep** and **Skip** buttons (with duplicate checks so events aren't added twice).
- **4 Clear Categories:** Organizes everything into **Medical**, **Family & Social**, **Bills & Errands**, and **Daily Routine** using icons, labels, and colors.
- **Calm Daily View:** Opens to **Today** and **This Week** with large text and optional reminders—no cramped monthly boxes.

### 7. My Data & Privacy Controls
- **You Stay in Charge:** Turn each message source and AI reading **ON or OFF** anytime, see a plain-language **"What AI Read"** log, download your data, or erase everything whenever you want.
- **No Unwanted Actions:** Nothing is added to your calendar, shared with family, or deleted unless you tap **Confirm**.
